We are pleased to welcome you to the redesigned [University of Maryland
Libraries Open Data website](https://opendata.lib.umd.edu). This launch marks
an important milestone in our ongoing efforts to improve access to the
Libraries’ open data collections and to better serve our research community
through modern, sustainable, and user-centered design.

## A Modern Platform for Open Data

The Open Data site has been fully migrated from its previous Sphinx framework
to **Hugo**, a modern static site generator that offers enhanced performance,
accessibility, and flexibility. This migration supports a more reliable and
efficient technical foundation and allows us to introduce several key
improvements:

* **Enhanced accessibility and usability:** The new interface provides a clear,
  consistent structure that supports equitable access and easy navigation for
  all users.
* **Improved performance and sustainability:** Hugo’s lightweight framework
  ensures faster load times and simplifies long-term maintenance.
* **Contemporary design and functionality:** The site’s updated layout reflects
  current web standards and enhances the overall user experience.
* **Expanded development support:** With Hugo’s active community and robust
  documentation, the Libraries’ development team can more effectively maintain
  and extend the platform.

## Supporting Open and Transparent Scholarship

This redevelopment advances the Libraries’ strategic commitment to promoting
open, transparent, and equitable scholarship. By strengthening the
infrastructure that supports our open data resources, we are furthering the
University of Maryland’s mission to partner in advancing the common good and
addressing humanity’s grand challenges.

## Looking Ahead

All existing datasets and documentation remain available through the new site,
and additional updates are planned to further enhance functionality and
engagement. Future improvements will include expanded dataset search
capabilities, integration with related repositories, and new opportunities for
collaboration and data sharing.

We invite you to explore the new website, discover the resources available, and
share your feedback as we continue to evolve this platform to meet the needs of
our users and partners.

Visit us at [opendata.lib.umd.edu](https://opendata.lib.umd.edu) and join us in
supporting open and connected research at the University of Maryland.
