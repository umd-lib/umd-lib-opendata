We are pleased to welcome you to the redesigned [University of Maryland
Libraries Open Data website](https://opendata.lib.umd.edu). The Open Data site
has been fully migrated from its previous Sphinx framework to
[Hugo](https://gohugo.io/), a modern static site generator that offers enhanced
performance, accessibility, and flexibility. This migration supports an updated
user interface and easier maintenance of the website.
