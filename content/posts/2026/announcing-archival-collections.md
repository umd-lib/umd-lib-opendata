We have added a new [Archival Collections](/services/archival-collections)
service. The University of Maryland Libraries collects and preserves archival
and manuscript collections of groups and individuals. The [Archival
Collections](https://archives.lib.umd.edu) database provides descriptions of
many of these collections.
