We have added a new [Archive-It Service](/services/archive-it), which is the
leading web archiving service for collecting and accessing cultural heritage on
the web. UMD routinely archives a variety of web content to enhance our unique
collections.

As of this posting the [University of Maryland
Organization](https://archive-it.org/organizations/408) contains these 6
Collections:

* [Historic Preservation Collection](https://archive-it.org/collections/1819)
* [Labor](https://archive-it.org/collections/5981)
* [Maryland State Document Collection](https://archive-it.org/collections/1826)
* [Special Collections](https://archive-it.org/collections/2269)
* [Special Collections in Performing
  Arts](https://archive-it.org/collections/4149)
* [University of Maryland](https://archive-it.org/collections/2410)
